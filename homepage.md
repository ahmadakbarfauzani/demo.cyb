## Front-End Product Requirements Document (PRD): CYB Home Page

**Objective:** Develop a responsive, premium front-end home page for "CYB" (Call Your Barber), a mobile barbershop based in Bali. The design is dark-themed and focuses on a strong visual hero section with clear booking calls to action.

### 1. Global UI Elements

* **Announcement Bar (Top Left):** Display a small text indicator reading "BOOKING OPEN - ACROSS BALI".


* **Quick Actions (Top Right):** Include an Instagram social icon next to a prominent green "BOOK NOW" button.


* **Main Navigation:**
* **Brand Logo (Left):** Display the CYB crest icon followed by the text "CYB".


* **Menu Links (Right):** Render horizontal text links for "STORY", "SERVICES", "GALLERY", "REVIEWS", "CONTACT", and "BOOK".




* **Floating Action Button (Bottom Right):** Implement a fixed, circular WhatsApp icon in green to allow immediate chat contact.



### 2. Hero Section

* **Background:** Implement a full-width, dark-tinted background image featuring a barber servicing a client.


* **Centered Brand Assets:** Display a large version of the CYB crest logo centrally above the text content.


* **Typography & Copy:**
* **Subheading:** Display "YOUR PERSONAL MOBILE" in small, tracked-out uppercase letters.


* **Headline:** Display "BARBERSHOP IN BALI" using a large, green display font.


* **Body Text:** Include a centered descriptive paragraph detailing the service ("CYB stands for Call Your Barber, the easiest cut you'll ever book...").




* **Primary Call to Action:** Render a centrally aligned "BOOK AN APPOINTMENT" button featuring a thin outlined border with small diamond accents on the left and right sides.



### 3. Scrolling & Navigation Aids

* **Scroll Indicator (Bottom Center):** Place the text "SCROLL" with a small downward-pointing diamond or arrow indicator at the bottom center of the viewport.



### 4. Theming & Styling Guidelines

* **Color Palette:** Utilize a predominantly dark background (black/dark gray) with stark white or light gray text for maximum contrast. Use a specific muted green for the primary headline ("BARBERSHOP IN BALI"), the top right "BOOK NOW" button, and the WhatsApp floating icon.


* **Visual Hierarchy:** Prioritize the large green headline and central logo to draw immediate user attention, utilizing negative space and dark image overlays to make the central text block stand out.
