## Front-End Product Requirements Document (PRD): CYB "Why Us" Section

**Objective:** Develop the front-end "Why Us" section (based on the design in "image_f481d7.jpg") to communicate the core value propositions of the CYB service. The section utilizes a dark theme, a prominent header, and a three-column feature grid with custom iconography and calls to action.

### 1. Section Header

* **Layout:** Centered alignment at the top of the section.


* **Top Divider:** Include a faint, horizontal line with a small diamond indicator centered perfectly at the very top of the section frame.


* **Kicker/Label:** Display the text "WHY CYB" in small, uppercase, gold/brown typography.


* **Headline:** Render the headline "BUILT AROUND YOUR CHAIR." prominently. The text "BUILT AROUND" must be displayed in a muted green, while "YOUR CHAIR." must be displayed in white.



### 2. Feature Grid (Three-Column Layout)

* **Layout Structure:** Implement a responsive three-column grid that aligns items centrally within each column.


* **Column 1: Precision Cuts**
* **Icon:** Display a hand-drawn style, gold/tan illustration of crossed hair clippers and a straight razor.


* **Title:** Display "PRECISION CUTS" in uppercase white text.


* **Body Text:** Display "Sharp fades, clean line-ups and proper beard work. Real barbering done at your place, with the same care you would get in a top chair." in centered, gray text.


* **Action Button:** Render an outlined, pill-shaped button containing the text "SEE SERVICES ↗".




* **Column 2: We Come To You**
* **Icon:** Display a hand-drawn style, gold/tan illustration of a folded map.


* **Title:** Display "WE COME TO YOU" in uppercase white text.


* **Body Text:** Display "No traffic and no waiting room. Your barber turns up on time across Bali with the full kit, ready to set up wherever suits you." in centered, gray text.


* **Action Button:** Render an outlined, pill-shaped button containing the text "WHERE WE GO ↗".




* **Column 3: Honest Prices**
* **Icon:** Display a hand-drawn style, gold/tan illustration of a tied money bag.


* **Title:** Display "HONEST PRICES" in uppercase white text.


* **Body Text:** Display "Clear, fixed prices with nothing hidden. Settle up by cash or QRIS once the cut is exactly how you want it." in centered, gray text.


* **Action Button:** Render an outlined, pill-shaped button containing the text "VIEW PRICES ↗".





### 3. Global Floating Elements

* **Floating Action Button (Bottom Right):** Persist the fixed, circular WhatsApp icon in bright green to facilitate immediate user contact.



### 4. Theming & Styling Guidelines

* **Background:** Maintain a solid dark (black/dark gray) background to match the established brand identity.


* **Typography & Color:** Use high-contrast white for primary titles and a readable mid-gray for paragraph copy to establish a clear visual hierarchy. Ensure the custom dual-color treatment (green and white) is applied accurately to the main headline.


* **Component Styling:** Buttons should feature a thin, subdued border with small, uppercase text and a consistent upward-right arrow (`↗`) indicating navigation. Icons must maintain a uniform gold/tan vintage aesthetic across all three columns.
