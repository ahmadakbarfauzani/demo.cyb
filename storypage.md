## Front-End Product Requirements Document (PRD): CYB Story Section

**Objective:** Develop the front-end "Story" section (based on the design in image_f4062f.jpg) to communicate the brand's mobile, premium value proposition. The section utilizes a dark theme, a continuous marquee, and a two-column layout to balance visual assets with copy.

### 1. Service Marquee (Top Banner)

* **Layout:** Implement a full-width horizontal band at the top of the section.


* **Content:** Display a repeating sequence of uppercase text highlighting core services: "PRECISION FADES", "BEARD SCULPTS", "STRAIGHT RAZOR SHAVES", and "AT YOUR DOOR".


* **Separators:** Separate each text item with a small, gold/tan four-pointed star or diamond icon.


* **Behavior:** Design this band to function as a continuously scrolling marquee or a static repeating banner across the viewport.



### 2. Main Content Area (Two-Column Layout)

* **Layout Structure:** Implement a two-column grid for desktop viewing.


* **Left Column (Visual Asset):**
* Display a large, vintage-style circular badge.


* The center must feature the intricate, intertwined "CYB" crest logo in gold/green tones.


* The center logo must be surrounded by circular, rotating text reading: "SHARP CUTS · AT YOUR HOME · BALI BARBER".




* **Right Column (Story Copy):**
* **Kicker/Label:** Display the word "STORY" in small, uppercase, reddish-brown text.


* **Headline:** Render the headline "THE CHAIR COMES TO YOU." using a large, uppercase, muted green display font.


* **Body Text:** Include three paragraphs of left-aligned, light gray/white text detailing the mobile service concept, specific Bali service areas (Uluwatu, Canggu, Ubud), and the unhurried, premium nature of the experience.





### 3. Bottom Divider

* **Visual Element:** Below the main content, render a faint, horizontal line featuring a small diamond indicator centered perfectly in the middle.



### 4. Theming & Styling Guidelines

* **Background:** Maintain a solid dark (black or very dark gray) background to ensure consistency with the overall brand identity.


* **Typography:** Use a distinct visual hierarchy, contrasting the bold green headline and colorful kicker against the muted, highly readable gray body text.
