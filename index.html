STORY

THE STUDIO COMES TO YOU.

CYB Body Art was born from a simple idea: premium body art shouldn't require you to fight Bali traffic or sit in a crowded waiting room. We designed a fully mobile, unhurried tattoo experience that brings the highest standard of artistry directly to your villa or hotel.

Currently servicing Uluwatu, Canggu, and Ubud, our expert artists arrive fully equipped with professional sterile tools, premium inks, and a commitment to precision.

Whether you want a delicate fine line piece or a complex custom sleeve, we ensure every detail is perfect, completely on your schedule.
